Round 2 Remodeling Business Contact
and Digital Footprint Research Report
(Batch 1: Rows 69 to 1083)
This report presents the first sequential research pass of the 1,802 small U.S. kitchen and
bathroom remodeling businesses listed in GEMINI_R2_TASK2.md, covering the first 106
businesses in exact table order from Row 69 through Row 1083 without skipping any entries1
.
Across this initial cohort—comprising all 5 Task A records, all 18 Task B records, and the first
83 Task C records—research uncovered or verified at least one primary digital asset (published
contact email, Facebook page, Instagram profile, or live/dead standalone domain) for 105 of the
106 businesses (99.1%), with 1 record (Row 1081, Empire Remodeling, whose input
Facebook link was a generic l.facebook.com/l.php redirect) yielding no valid social profile,
website, or published email after multi-directory verification1
.
Across the 106 researched businesses, 19 verified business-published email addresses
were confirmed with exact source URLs and verbatim quotes of 20 words or fewer, excluding
people-search and email-finder aggregators such as TruePeopleSearch, VeriPages,
RocketReach, and InstantCheckmate4
. In addition, 9 live standalone websites were
discovered or confirmed active—including 5 businesses in Tasks A and B whose previously
dead domains were either restored or migrated to new domains (such as Luxury Custom
Kitchens INC migrating from capirocorp.com to luxurycustomkitchens.com, and Grace
Remodeling & Construction operating at gracecandr.com) and 4 Task C businesses operating
active websites not previously linked to their Google Business Profiles9
. Furthermore, 48 owner
or contact names were identified across the batch, including 38 formal owners or principals
verified via Better Business Bureau (BBB) profiles, state contractor licensing boards (California
CSLB, Washington L&I, Louisiana LSLBC, Arizona ROC, Pennsylvania OAG), federal FMCSA
filings, and local newspaper or showroom records, alongside 10 additional operator or staff
names identified via customer reviews and social handles14
.
