9. kurtiskitchen.com (7/10) Bold red family-owned remodeler

Review block: red quote card with stars, then a rating strip of Google 5.0, Houzz 4.3, Angi 4.3, BBB A+ with logos and big numerals plus 'Read More Reviews' button; Showroom locator: map with pins on the left, accordion of showroom cities on the right with address, hours, phone, Get Directions and Schedule Now; Mobile sticky 3-button bar: Call, Contact, Menu with icons in solid red; Strong third-generation family story with real numbers ('100,000 kitchens and 100,000 bathrooms')

10. henrybuilt.com (7/10) Minimal architectural luxury

Full-bleed kitchen photo hero with only a letterspaced 'HENRYBUILT / CRAFT + INNOVATION' and a showroom city list, scroll-down chevron; Restraint: one line of copy, photography carries the luxury; Showroom cities as a quiet trust and location signal

11. studio-mcgee.com (7/10) Warm heritage editorial design house

Two-line heading pairs: roman word over a lighter descriptive line ('New Heritage / A design house rooted in tradition.') in italic; Founder block on espresso: layered collage of founder photo and material swatches, serif heading, short story, outlined About button; Press quote carousel: italic serif quote from The New York Times over a wood-grain photo with the masthead logo below; Portfolio carousel with oversized center image and smaller side images on slate, Prev/Next squares and See More Projects
