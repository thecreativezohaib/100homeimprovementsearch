## Tile Questions for Wolston

**What are your hours?**  
Wolston Tile Designs is open Monday through Friday from 8 AM to 5 PM and closed on weekends, according to Google. Call (580) 467-4399 during those hours to talk about your project, or send a message through their Facebook or Instagram page.

**Do you handle the plumbing?**  
No. In their own Facebook post they explain that they are not licensed plumbers and leave that work to plumbers. That is why their finished shower photos often show no trim kits, which a plumber installs after the tile is done. Plan for a plumber as part of a shower project.

**Can I design my own shower?**  
Yes. A June 2026 Facebook post shows a finished custom shower that the homeowners designed themselves, which Wolston then built. If you already have tile, a layout or photos you like, bring them to the first conversation so the plan starts from your ideas.

**How messy is tile demolition?**  
Tearing out old tile is dusty work, but reviewers say Wolston pays close attention to keeping dust from getting into everything and keeps a very neat workspace. Ask how they will protect the rest of your home before demolition begins on your project.

**Are estimates free, and are you insured?**  
Free estimates, insurance, warranty terms and years in business are not published on Google or Facebook, so ask when you call. Reviewers do say the final price matched the original contract and that the prices were very fair. Get your contract in writing before work starts.
