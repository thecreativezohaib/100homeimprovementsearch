## Countertop questions

**Can I see the slab before I buy?**

Yes. Customers choose their granite during their visit to the shop instead of picking from a photo. The owners say selecting stone in person is an important part of the process, and project managers like Melissa help you find the right slabs for your space.

**What materials do you work with?**

Melling specializes in custom natural stone and quartz countertops for kitchens and baths. Past projects include granite islands, veined natural stone tops, stone shower walls and a carved stone range hood surround. Call or visit to see what is in the shop now.

**Do you handle bathrooms or only kitchens?**

Both. The shop focuses on countertops for kitchens and baths, and one customer used Melling for all the stone in a whole-house remodel, including a walk-in shower lined with large stone slabs. Bring your bathroom measurements or plans when you visit.

**What are your hours?**

The shop is open Monday to Friday from 9 AM to 5 PM and closed on Saturday and Sunday. You can call (435) 867-0099 during those hours or email corri@mellinggranite.com to ask a question or plan a visit to look at slabs.

**Can you add outlets or other details in the stone?**

Ask during your visit. The shop has shown Bocci plugs, a sleek, modern outlet, side by side with a regular plug on its Facebook page. Reviewers say Melling settles all the specifics up front, so details like outlets and sinks are planned before cutting.
