## Countertop questions

**Do you do the installation yourselves?**

Yes. Reviewers describe the Countertop Shop crew handling everything from templating to fabrication and installation, including heavy granite that took several people and special equipment to set safely. Ask during your visit how scheduling works for your job and who will be on site.

**Can you handle tricky kitchens?**

One kitchen had unlevel cabinets, a large galley sink and three load-bearing posts. The crew cut the granite around each post, braced the bar, desk and sink, and placed the seams where they are hard to notice. Bring photos of your layout to the showroom.

**What else do you offer besides countertops?**

Backsplash tile, sinks, faucets, cabinetry, flooring and demolition. The showroom displays these together, and reviewers mention the wide selection of products and faucets. That makes it easier to plan a full kitchen or bathroom update in one place, with one team to call.

**What are your showroom hours?**

Google lists the showroom as open Monday to Friday from 8:30 AM to 5 PM, closed on Saturday and Sunday. Call (580) 234-7131 or email info@enidcountertopshop.com to ask a question or plan your visit. Bring measurements or photos of your cabinets so the team can help you compare options.

**Will my whole job be installed on the same day?**

That depends on the job and on every slab being ready. When you schedule, ask whether all of your pieces, such as the kitchen and a bathroom vanity, will be installed on the same day, so you can plan any time off work around it.
