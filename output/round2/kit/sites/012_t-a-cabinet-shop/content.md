## Questions homeowners ask

### Where is T & A Cabinet Shop?

The shop and showroom are at 148 Bean St, Soddy-Daisy, TN 37379. You can visit during opening hours to look at cabinet displays, door samples and countertop samples, and to talk with the office about a new kitchen or a house you are building.

### What are your hours?

Monday 9 AM to 3 PM, Tuesday through Thursday 9 AM to 4 PM, and Friday 9 AM to 3 PM. The shop is closed Saturday and Sunday. Outside those hours you can email tacabinets@comcast.net with your question or plans and pick up the conversation by phone during shop hours.

### Do you build the cabinets yourselves?

Yes. Cabinet orders are put on the shop work schedule and built at the Bean Street shop in Soddy-Daisy, then delivered and installed by the T & A install crew. Ask the office where your order sits on the current schedule when you place it.

### Do you work with builders on new homes?

Yes. Many customers who review T & A were building a new house, and the shop worked alongside their builder or general contractor. The team measures on site, builds the cabinets in the shop and installs them as the house comes together.

### How do I get a price for cabinets?

Call (423) 332-5635 or email tacabinets@comcast.net with your plans or rough measurements, or bring them to the showroom on a weekday. The team will arrange to come out and measure, so the price you get is based on your actual kitchen and layout.
