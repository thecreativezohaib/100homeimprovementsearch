## How a custom order works

1. **Call or stop in.** Call (814) 942-1000 or visit 110 Frankstown Rd on a weekday between 7:30 AM and 4 PM.
2. **Bring a sample or measurements.** Bring a piece of the trim you need matched, or the measurements and floor heights for a threshold or transition.
3. **Talk through the details.** The crew offers ideas and solutions. Share every detail of the job, since price depends on how complex the piece is.
4. **Milled in the shop.** Your moulding, trim or threshold is made in the woodshop in the hardwood you choose: oak, poplar, cherry, maple or walnut.
5. **Collect your order.** Pick up your finished pieces at the store, along with any stock trim, hardware or lumber you need to finish the job.

## Questions customers ask

**Can you match old trim in my house?**

Yes. The shop has custom matched vintage baseboard trim for customers restoring older homes. Bring a sample piece or a clear profile to the store at 110 Frankstown Rd, and the crew can talk you through wood species, lengths and what it will take to mill it.

**Which woods do you work with?**

Custom mouldings are made in oak, poplar, cherry, maple and walnut. The store also sells hardwood lumber surfaced two sides (D2S) or four sides (D4S), plywood and veneer, so you can buy matching stock for the rest of your project.

**Do you sell cabinets and countertops?**

The store's awning lists countertops and cabinet hardware along with hardwood flooring and stair parts, and cabinets are in the company's name. Call (814) 942-1000 or stop in to ask what is available for your kitchen and how custom wood pieces from the shop can finish it off.

**What are your hours?**

Modern Cabinet & Construction is open Monday through Friday from 7:30 AM to 4 PM and closed Saturday and Sunday. You can also email moderncabinet@atlanticbbn.net with photos or measurements of what you need, then follow up by phone during store hours.

**How is a custom piece priced?**

Price depends on the details: wood species, length, profile and how complex the job is. The shop has explained to customers that a quote for a simple piece changes once more complex details come in, so share every detail up front for the most accurate number.
