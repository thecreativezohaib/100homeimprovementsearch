**Are you licensed and insured?**  
Yes. Empire Cabinets is licensed and insured, and we have over 20 years of experience in new home construction, remodels, kitchen design and wood furniture. Ask us for the details of our licence and insurance when we talk about your project.

**Can you add cabinets that match my existing ones?**  
Yes. In a Hayes Town house, Ryan added new cabinets, modified the existing cabinetry and blended the styles so they met the owner's quality standards. He also adjusted drawers and added pull-outs to the older cabinets while he was there.

**Do you build islands?**  
Yes, new and enlarged. One customer had her island enlarged and the cabinets painted, and another had a new island built that looks like the custom piece she hoped for. Tell us the size you want and how you plan to use it.
