## How a custom cabinet project works

1. **Call and talk it over.** Phone (254) 709-5497 on a weekday between 8 AM and 6 PM and tell us which rooms you want cabinets for.
2. **Measurements.** We visit, take careful measurements and go over what you want, from door style to finish and storage needs.
3. **Drawing and quote.** You get a drawing and a written price for your cabinets, so you can review options and add-ons before anything is built.
4. **Build, finish and install.** Your cabinets are built, painted or stained and varnished, then installed to fit the space exactly.
