**Are estimates free?**

Yes. Free estimates are printed right on the J&M business card. Call (786) 608-0337 or email jmfloorandbathroomconst@gmail.com with a few photos of your bathroom or floor, and we will set up a time to come out, look at it and talk about what you want.

**My shower leaks. Can you fix it?**

Yes. A broken shower pan can let water get behind the walls for years before anyone notices. J&M replaces failed shower pans and fixes the leaks before any new tile goes up. If you see stains, soft spots or mold around your shower, call (786) 608-0337 and ask the crew to take a look.

**Do you replace pipes and drains?**

**When can I reach you?**

We are open Monday through Friday from 6 AM to 9 PM and on Saturday from 9 AM to 2 PM. We are closed on Sunday. Early and evening hours mean you can usually call before or after your own workday.
