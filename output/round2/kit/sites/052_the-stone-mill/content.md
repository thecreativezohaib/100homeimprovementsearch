## How a countertop job works

1. **Call or visit**: Call (601) 909-5772 or stop by Rose Farm Rd on a weekday between 8:30 AM and 5 PM to talk through your project.
2. **Pick your stone**: Choose between granite, other natural stone and quartz. Staff can give advice and recommendations on what suits your kitchen or bath.
3. **Measure and cut**: Your counters are measured and cut to fit, including the holes for sinks and faucets so your plumber can finish the hookup.
4. **Install and walk through**: The tops are set in your home. Look everything over with the installer and point out anything you want checked before they leave.
