1. **Visit or call**: Call (508) 278-6551 or stop by the shop on Quaker Hwy, Monday to Friday, 7:30 AM to 4 PM.
2. **Measure and quote**: The team gets all the specs for your room first, then gives you a quote based on what they measured.
