## From slab yard to installed countertops

1. **Visit the warehouse**: Stop by 320 S Mt Pleasant Rd, Monday to Friday 8 AM to 5 PM or Saturday 9 AM to 1 PM, and walk the slabs.
2. **In-house estimate**: C&M has offered free in-house estimates. Call (901) 854-7007 to set one up and confirm the details.
3. **Design cabinets and choose stone**: Work out your cabinet layout and pick the exact slab you want for your kitchen, bath or bar.
4. **Fabricate and install**: Your stone is fabricated and installed, and if new cabinets are part of the job, the tops go in after the cabinets.
5. **Check it over**: Walk through the finished job with the installer, and point out anything you want checked before they leave.
