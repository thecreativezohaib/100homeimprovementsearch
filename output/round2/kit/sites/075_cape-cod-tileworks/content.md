## Tile that looks like it belongs on Cape Cod

Cape Cod Tileworks has done custom tile in Harwich, MA for decades. In February 2022 the business marked 27 years in its Harwich building, and it still designs and installs tile projects. Its Facebook page sums it up in three words: design and installation.

The work is easy to spot. Pebble backsplashes with ceramic fish and a mermaid swimming across them. A tile map of Cape Cod set over a bathtub. Pebble fireplace surrounds and patios that used to be bare cement slabs. Owner Lane Meehan is a RISD graduate who helps you pick the right tile, and Tom Meehan, a second-generation installer, sets it.

## How a tile project comes together

1. **Call Lane**: Call (508) 246-6324 and describe the room, the feel you want and any pieces you already love.
2. **Design and select**: Lane draws on her RISD design training to help you choose tile, colors and any custom mosaic or mural elements.
3. **Lay out the art**: Custom mosaic pieces, pebbles and glass are laid out by hand on paper before they go on the wall, as their photos show.
4. **Install**: Tom, a second-generation installer, sets the tile. Or your own contractor installs it, as several customers have done.

## Questions about custom tile

**Can you design a custom mosaic for my house?**

Yes. Custom work is what Cape Cod Tileworks is known for: pebble backsplashes with ceramic fish and a mermaid, a tile map of Cape Cod, carved owls and flowers among river pebbles. Lane designs each piece, and they have written that they look forward to recreating a look for a new customer.

**Do you install, or only sell tile?**

Both. The Facebook page describes the business as design and installation. Tom Meehan, a second-generation tile installer, sets tile, and one family had their kitchen backsplash installed after buying tile for two bathrooms. Other customers have their own contractor install the tile Lane helps them choose.

**Is the Harwich shop still open?**

In February 2022, after 27 years, Lane wrote that she had walked out of the building for the last time, and that they are still here to help design your next project. Google still lists 705 Main St, Harwich, so call (508) 246-6324 before visiting.

**Do you work with contractors?**

Yes. Schoolhouse Construction Inc. says it has worked with Cape Cod Tileworks for many years on many kitchen and bathroom remodeling projects. Lane can help your builder pick materials, and the Facebook page shows before and after bathrooms done with Schoolhouse.

**Can tile go outdoors?**

Yes. In August 2021 Cape Cod Tileworks shared a project where a plain cement slab became a tiled outdoor living space. They wrote that tile is not just for inside the house. Call to talk about patios, outdoor showers or other outdoor ideas for your home.
