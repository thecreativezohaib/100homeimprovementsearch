## How refinishing works

1. **Free quote**: Call (254) 523-5754 for a free quote. Frank can come out to look at the tub, tile or countertop and give you an estimate.
2. **Pick a color**: Choose from many colors, or keep it classic white. Ask about the 5 year warranty for your job.
3. **Prep and mask**: The area around the tub or counter is masked off with paper and tape so only the surface being refinished gets coated.
4. **Refinish**: The surface is resurfaced in place. Tubs often take just hours and most tile jobs are done within two days.
5. **Cure and enjoy**: Once the finish has cured, the tape comes off and your tub, tile or counter is ready to use.
