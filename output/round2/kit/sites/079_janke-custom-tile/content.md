**Do you offer free estimates?**

Yes. Janke Custom Tile invites people to call for a free estimate. Customers say Kevin comes to the house to take measurements and give a quote, and he has scheduled work around a customer's day off. Call or message (989) 475-5452 to set it up.

**Can you build a curbless shower?**

Yes. Janke Custom Tile has shared photos of subfloors prepped for upcoming curbless shower pans. A curbless shower has no step at the entry, which many people want for easier access. Ask about it at your estimate so the floor can be planned for it.

**What kinds of tile do you install?**

Ceramic, porcelain and natural stone tile, including marble. Projects include 12 by 24 polished porcelain walls, hexagon marble mosaic shower floors, 24 by 24 checkered porcelain floors and 8 by 36 porcelain plank flooring. Janke also offers custom design and layout.

**Can you fix a tile job that was done wrong?**

Yes. In 2022 Janke Custom Tile removed 1,700 square feet of six-month-old wood-look tile that had been installed incorrectly and replaced it with dark cherry porcelain plank tile. Call with photos of the problem and Kevin can tell you what it needs.

**Is your work covered?**

A September 2024 Janke Custom Tile post describes coverage for installation issues related to their workmanship, such as loose tiles or grout cracking, and mentions Kerdi waterproofing. Ask for the full written terms at your estimate so you know exactly what is covered and for how long.
