## How a remodel with Kelly works

1. **Talk it through.** Call (636) 697-0380 or email kelly@kellyskandb.com. Kelly listens to what you want and lays out the plan and timing from the start.
2. **Choose products.** Kelly recommends cabinets, counters, fixtures and finishes that look good and work well, then you decide together.
3. **Demo and rebuild.** The old room is gutted. Walls and appliance locations change where needed, then floors, lighting and cabinets go in.
4. **Finish and walkthrough.** Appliances and fixtures are installed and you walk the finished room, on the timeline you were given at the start.
