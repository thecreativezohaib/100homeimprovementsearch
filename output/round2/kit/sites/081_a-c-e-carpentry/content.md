## How a Remodel With A.C.E Works

1. **Call Carl.** Call (540) 699-3098 Monday to Friday 7 AM to 9 PM, or Saturday 7 AM to 5 PM, and describe the room.
2. **Walk the space.** Carl looks at the room, including any problem work left by someone else, and talks through what needs to change.
3. **Demolition and rough-in.** Where needed, the room is gutted and the plumbing, electrical and drywall are redone before finishes go in.
4. **Finishes.** Cabinets, counters, flooring, trim and fixtures are installed, with the crew keeping you informed as they go.
5. **Walkthrough.** You walk the finished room with Carl, check the details together and point out anything you would like adjusted.
