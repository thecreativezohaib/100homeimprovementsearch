Kitchen and bathroom remodeling is the core, from new cabinets and counters to master bath redesigns. We also remodel basements, laundry rooms and living spaces, build accent and fireplace walls, and handle carpentry, flooring and painting. If your project is not listed, call and describe it.

**Do you meet with me before the work starts?**

Yes. Von, the owner, comes out and walks through the areas that need work with you. After that you get an estimate and a start date. One client says the workers then arrived promptly on the day the job was scheduled to begin, exactly as planned.

Yes. One client hired us for the lower level of her home. We turned a dark, drab mancave covered in wood paneling into a bright entertainment space, created a private office, and revamped her laundry room with epoxy flooring and additional storage shelves.

We are open seven days a week, Monday through Sunday, from 8 AM to 5:30 PM. Call (708) 743-2081, or email aiorsvcs@gmail.com with a short description and a few photos of the space, and Von will get back to you to set up a walk-through.
