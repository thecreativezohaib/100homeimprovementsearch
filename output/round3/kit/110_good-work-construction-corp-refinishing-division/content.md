For many tubs, yes. One customer planned to buy a new tub and was glad he chose refinishing instead, because his tub looks brand new. Refinishing skips the demolition, plumbing and tile repair that come with pulling out a tub. Send photos and we will tell you honestly which way makes sense.

**Can you fix a tub that is chipping?**

Yes. One master bath tub had been chipping and getting worse over several years before the homeowner called us, and she says she could not be happier with the results. The before and after photos on this page show another chipped tub we refinished.
