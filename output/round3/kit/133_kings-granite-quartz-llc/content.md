## Questions homeowners ask

**Should I choose granite or quartz?**

It depends on the look and feel you want. Granite is natural stone, so each slab has its own pattern and color variation. Quartz is engineered, so the color and pattern stay more even across every piece. Many homeowners decide after seeing both. Call us and we will talk through the options for your kitchen.

**What should I have ready when I call?**

A rough sketch or photos of your current counters help a lot, along with the approximate lengths of each run, where the sink and cooktop sit and whether you want a new sink. If you have a color or stone in mind, send a picture of that as well.
